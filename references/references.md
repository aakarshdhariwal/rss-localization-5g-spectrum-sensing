# References

Third-party papers, theses, and articles collected and cited during the literature
review for this thesis. They are included here, organized by topic, for context and
easy cross-referencing alongside the code and report. All copyrights remain with the
original authors and publishers; these copies are included for non-commercial,
academic, and portfolio purposes only.

Entries marked **[cited]** are directly referenced in the thesis bibliography
(`report/RSS_Based_Localization_using_Distributed_Spectrum_Sensing_for_5G_Networks.pdf`).
Titles marked *(title from filename)* belong to two scanned/image-only PDFs whose title
page could not be text-extracted to verify the exact author list; the filename-derived
title is accurate but the author list below was not independently confirmed for those
two entries.

## A note on excluded material

`Report/RSS_thesis_idea.pdf` in the original project folder was found, on inspection,
to be an unrelated PhD dissertation — "Received Signal Strength based Localization in
Wireless Sensor Networks" by Ranjan Kumar Mahapatra, National Institute of Technology
Karnataka, Surathkal, November 2017 — used as background/inspiration reading rather
than the author's own writing, and it is not cited anywhere in the thesis bibliography.
It has been left out of this repository to avoid misattributing a third party's
doctoral dissertation. See the top-level README for further detail.
